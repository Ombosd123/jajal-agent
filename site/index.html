How it works — the rules, in the open

  • 10,000 shares. 1 share = 100,000 JAL if the sale completes. Maximum 11 shares per wallet.
  • First 1,000 shares are free — 1 per Agent ID holder, first come first served, claimed on this page.
  • Then paid tiers: the price rises every 1,000 shares — 0.0001 BNB per share in tier 1, up to 0.0009 BNB in tier 9. Paid shares open only after the free quota is gone.
  • If all 10,000 shares are taken (the deadline does not matter): the pool opens — from Dec 4, 2026, 17:00 UTC at the earliest, because the contract gates the one-time finalize step until then even if the sale sells out earlier. 50% of the BNB raised, together with 1,000,000,000 JAL, seeds a JAL/WBNB pool on PancakeSwap V2 and the LP tokens are burned — locked permanently. The other 50% is returned to buyers as cashback when they claim. Buyers then claim their JAL plus their cashback, released by the contract in waves by share position (see the claim panel below).
  • If the target is missed by the deadline (Dec 5, 2026, 16:59 UTC): every buyer can withdraw a 100% refund of what they paid. Free shares simply expire. No JAL is ever minted in this case.
  • Token supply — minted once, only if the sale completes: exactly 2,500,000,000 JAL. Of that: 1,000,000,000 JAL (40%) for participant claims, 1,000,000,000 JAL (40%) into the liquidity pool, 200,000,000 JAL (8%) team allocation under the vesting schedule below, and 300,000,000 JAL (12%) held by the check-in contract as a bonus reserve (see Daily Check-In). If the sale fails, no JAL is minted at all — the supply stays zero.
  • Team allocation, disclosed: the 200,000,000 JAL team share is held by the sale contract as a vesting balance — it is not sent to any wallet at launch. It unlocks on a fixed schedule — nothing for the first 30 days after the pool opens, then released linearly over the next 90 days — and can only ever be withdrawn by the team beneficiary address named in the contract.
  • Optional daily check-in: a separate contract lets Agent ID holders check in once a day for a 0.0001 BNB fee, collect 7 check-in days (they don't have to be in a row) for a soulbound badge, and — after the pool opens — claim a one-time bonus from the 300,000,000 JAL reserve. It is entirely optional and never required for the sale. Full rules are in the Daily Check-In panel below.
  • Every rule above is enforced by the contract code itself. There is no owner, no admin key, and no withdraw function — nobody can change the rules or take the funds, including the team.

⚠ Risk warning — read this first

$JAL is a speculative community token. It has no guaranteed value, and if a pool opens its price can fall — including to zero. Cashback and refunds are simply the sale rules written above; they are not a promise of profit or returns of any kind. Only take part with money you can afford to lose entirely, and verify the contract yourself before sending any BNB.

How check-in works — all rules in the open

  • Optional, once a day. Any Agent ID holder can check in once per day (UTC) for a fee of 0.0001 BNB. There is no obligation to take part, and skipping a day costs nothing — your check-in days add up cumulatively and never reset.
  • Where the fee goes: 50% is paid to the team wallet named in the contract (project operating costs — disclosed here in the open). The other 50% stays in the check-in contract as pool funds. After the sale completes and the pool opens, those held funds are added to the JAL/WBNB liquidity pool. If the sale fails instead, every check-in participant can claim back exactly their own pool share — 50% of the total check-in fees they personally paid — in a single claim through this same contract. Team share is non-refundable; pool share is refundable if the sale fails.
  • 7 check-in days badge: check in on 7 different days (UTC) — they do not have to be in a row — and the contract mints you a check-in badge NFT. Badge earning closes on Dec 8, 2026 (16:59 UTC). The badge is soulbound — it cannot be transferred or sold.
  • Badge bonus: badge holders can claim a one-time bonus equal to 35% of their final JAL allocation from the sale (their shares × 100,000 JAL × 35%). The bonus is paid out of the 300,000,000 JAL check-in reserve, first come first served — once the reserve is empty, no further bonuses are paid. Bonus claims open only after the sale completes and the pool opens, because that is when the reserve is minted, and they stay open until the program ends on Dec 14, 2026 (16:59 UTC). Any reserve still left after that date is burned — the contract's burnRemainder() function is permissionless, so anyone can trigger that burn once the program end date written in the contract has passed.
  • Check-in starts on launch day, together with the sale, and stays open until Dec 8, 2026 (16:59 UTC) — a few days past the sale deadline. If the sale misses its target on Dec 5, check-in stops on Dec 5 instead, and the pool refunds described above open. The check-in contract has no owner and no withdraw function outside the rules above.
